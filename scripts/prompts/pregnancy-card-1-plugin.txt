Using the “Integrated Pregnancy, Delivery and Postnatal Care Tracker”, build an enrollment plugin called “Pregnancy Journey Card” and add it to the program's Capture enrollment page.

For the pregnancy that is open, show: the current gestational age and due date (from the last menstrual period, or from the ultrasound when the two differ by more than 7 days, saying which was used); antenatal contacts done against the WHO 8-contact schedule (12, 20, 26, 30, 34, 36, 38 and 40 weeks), which are overdue and which are not due yet; blood pressure and haemoglobin over time with the warning limits (140/90, and Hb below 11); which tests are still missing (HIV, syphilis, blood group, and an ultrasound before 24 weeks); danger signs and open referrals; the risk classification; the next appointment; and, once she has delivered, the delivery and postnatal summary.

Add buttons that open the right form for the next step (a new follow-up visit, lab results, delivery or a postnatal visit). The card must update when I switch to another person or pregnancy and after I save a form, and it must keep separate pregnancies of the same woman separate.

The program has very few records, so first add about 30 fictional test pregnancies at the program's facilities, covering early pregnancy, overdue contacts, missing tests, high blood pressure, anaemia, a delivered case with postnatal visits, and a woman with two pregnancies. Don't leave any test record saved that the program's rules reject.

Build, install and place the plugin, test it on several of these pregnancies, and share the link and what you checked.
