Create a dashboard plugin called “Malaria Focus Response Explorer” using the malaria focus investigation program.

Show investigation points and response boundaries wherever real locations exist. Overlay malaria cases where their locations are available, but do not assume nearby cases belong to a focus. Avoid double-counting people or households across repeated responses; explain when a reliable unique total cannot be calculated.

Make the plugin follow dashboard filters, support export and printing, and keep its settings after reloading. Add two copies to a demonstration dashboard with different settings and confirm that changing one does not affect the other.
